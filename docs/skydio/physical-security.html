Modern physical security demands speed, coverage, and confidence that traditional methods can’t deliver. Skydio Physical Security meets that challenge with dock-based, autonomous drones that launch in seconds to verify alarms, patrol perimeters, and deliver real-time visibility across every site—enhancing coverage and lowering costs across your operation.

From alert to response in seconds.

How automated patrols, live video, and AI detection keep your team a step ahead.

Initiate

Missions start on a pre-planned schedule or launch automatically from Intrusion Detection System (IDS) alerts, access control events, or third-party sensors. A remote operator authorizes each flight from a central location.

Patrol

Flights cover the full site day or night, following pre-planned, or on-demand routes. Spotlights and speakers add visible deterrence wherever needed. Drones are on scene in seconds.

Monitor

Stream video and best-in-class thermal directly into your Video Management System (VMS), command center, or operator consoles—giving instant situational awareness from any angle.

Detect

AI-powered analytics detect people, vehicles, and unusual activity in real time, filtering out nuisance alerts and surfacing real threats.

Act

With threats confirmed, teams have instant context and respond with confidence. Send a live streaming ReadyLink via text or email to distribution lists aligned with reporting rules or organization hierarchy.

X10

The best sensors for a drone this small, piloted by the most advanced AI in the sky.

X10 combines high-resolution visual and radiometric thermal sensors with advanced AI autonomy in a rugged, backpack-portable drone that deploys in under a minute.

X10 combines high-resolution visual and radiometric thermal sensors with advanced AI autonomy in a rugged, backpack-portable drone. Capture critical data for emergency response, asset inspection, site security, situational awareness, and mapping, even in complex or GPS-denied environments.

Dock for X10

Drones at the ready for 911 calls, incident management, site security, asset inspection and emergency response.

Dock for X10 positions drones where the work happens, protecting and charging the aircraft between flights so teams can launch scheduled or on-demand missions without an onsite pilot.

Dock for X10 positions drones where the work happens so teams can remotely launch scheduled or on-demand missions without an onsite pilot. It protects and charges X10 between flights and keeps the aircraft ready for incident response, inspections, security patrols, and continuous site operations.

R10

For tight quarters and total confidence. See what's inside from the outside.

R10 is a compact indoor drone built to navigate confined, cluttered, and zero-light spaces—from a building interior to a tank or tunnel—so you can risk the machine, not the person.

R10 is a compact indoor drone built to navigate confined, cluttered, and zero-light environments. AI-driven autonomy, onboard lighting, communications, live streaming, and automated backtracking give teams visibility in hard-to-reach spaces before anyone has to step inside and take the risk.

F10 Lightrunner

Go farther, get there faster and stay on scene longer.

F10 Lightrunner is an autonomous, dock-based fixed-wing drone built for long-range, rapid-response missions that reach well beyond the coverage of a multirotor aircraft.

F10 Lightrunner is an autonomous, dock-based fixed-wing drone built for long-range, rapid-response missions. It flies up to 100 mph, with a coverage radius of 30 miles, with an endurance of 2 hours or more, giving teams fast aerial access and sustained overwatch across large and remote operating areas.

MegaDock

Autonomous launch and recovery for an always-ready F10 fleet.

MegaDock is the autonomous docking station for F10 Lightrunner, housing up to five aircraft. Robotic Takeoff and Landing (RTOL) launches and recovers them with no runway or crew on site.

MegaDock keeps up to five F10s charged, secure, and ready for rapid response inside a weather-sealed enclosure. Its Robotic Takeoff and Landing (RTOL) system launches and recovers each aircraft automatically—no runway or ground crew required—so teams remotely cover concurrent incidents and maintain coverage through handoffs.
